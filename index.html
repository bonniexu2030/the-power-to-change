BEFORE YOU BEGIN

The past is not
as distant as it seems.

This project explores selected events from the Civil Rights and Women's Rights Movements in the United States. Scenarios are designed for reflection, not to suggest that one choice could have single-handedly changed history. Historical context and individual experiences matter.
