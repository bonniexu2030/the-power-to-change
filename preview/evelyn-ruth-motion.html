Animation study — what you're reviewing

This four-stage, silent preview studies the approved emotional arc: a short reassuring embrace, Ruth's anticipation as the bus approaches, Evelyn's unobserved proud glance, then a dissolve to both mother and daughter seated together inside. The lighting moves from muted December gray to gentle warmth. You can pause, replay, or step through each shot.

Not the final keyframe paintings: this lightweight preview uses the original approved portrait files and a temporary layered illustrated street/bus environment to test motion and pacing. The hug and facial expressions will be more natural once the reviewed painted keyframes are implemented. There is no voice-over, score, or unnecessary on-screen dialogue here.
