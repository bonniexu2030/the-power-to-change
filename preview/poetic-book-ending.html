What this mockup tests

An aged-paper literary page, four short ink-written lines, a glowing public-domain historical quotation, and a burgundy antique book closing to reveal The Power to Change. The final nine endings would share the book structure but have different writing and character-specific page ornaments.

Review draft, not a final game scene. This page doesn't replace any game ending, journal, or historical material. The original prose above is provisional, and the historical quote comes from Frances Ellen Watkins Harper's 1866 speech.

Audio and accessibility

The animation plays silently by default, so it cannot surprise visitors with sound. “Hear temporary browser narration” lets you test the rhythm with your device's built-in speech voice; this is not a selected narrator or a finished recording. The finished game can use an approved narrator and matching subtitles. Pause, replay, and skip are available.
